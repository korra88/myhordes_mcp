**Town Elder — your MyHordes advisor**
Ask in plain words, in any language. In a channel, mention me and I answer in a thread; carry on there without mentioning me. In a DM just write; `/new` starts a fresh conversation. Answers take up to a minute.

**Town**
- "How is the town doing?" · "What changed since this morning?"
- "Are we safe tonight?" · "Attack forecast for the next days"
- "What happened last night?"
- "Do we have enough planks and metal in the bank?"

**Building**
- "What should we build today?"
- "Which blueprints should we read first?"
- "How much AP went into construction each day?"

**Expeditions**
- "Path to [14|2] and back, avoiding zombies"
- "Supplies for a trip to [5|1] for 2 people with 5 CP"
- "Route that brings 2 defense items home"
- "What is at [3|-4]?" · "Who is outside, and where?"
- Paste the expedition list and ask "who is outside but not on it?": I also list who is on it but still in town, and count the scrappers (1 tile out) apart

**Game rules**
- "How does thirst work?" · "What does the Tamer's dog carry?"

**Your town**
`/set-my-town 8299` sets your default town (Fata Morgana town ID). To check it, ask me "which town am I on?". For one question about another town, name it: "in town 8299, …".

**Good to know**
- Read-only: I never act in the game for you.
- Data comes from Fata Morgana: it is as fresh as the last map update a citizen made.
- After the attack (around midnight) the data shows the new day only once a citizen updates the map.
- "Remember …" stores a fact for every conversation; "forget …" removes it.
- Wrong answer? Tell me what was wrong: it is logged for the maintainer.
