# Player Behavior Patterns

Community-observed behavioral patterns that affect how to interpret live town data.

## Timezones & Return Times

- **American players** (US timezones) typically return to town around **22:00–23:00 CET**. If citizens are outside the walls in the afternoon or early evening, do not panic — they are very likely to return before the midnight attack. Only raise the alarm if they are still out past 23:30 CET.
- **European players** tend to return earlier (18:00–21:00 CET). If a European player is still out at 22:30, that is more unusual and worth flagging.

## General Patterns

- Players who have been active all day (high build count, items taken from bank) are likely to return for the attack — their engagement signals intent.
- Players who haven't been seen in the game logs for 24+ hours may be absent and should be treated as a risk factor for the watch.
