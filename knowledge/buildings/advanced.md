## Notes

- **The Big Rebuild + Underground city** are the biggest AP sinks in the game. Coordinate citizen AP across multiple days. Blueprint rare both.
- **Dump Upgrade** (BP-VR) — requires Garbage Dump built first. 65 def + best H ratio in game after Gas Gun.
- **Sprinkler System** (BP-R, +185 def) — best single water-consumer. Needs water surplus secured first.
- **False Town** — 400 def, 400 AP, BP-R. CS built it day 15. TV skipped. Worth it if coalition has AP to spare.
- **Water-consuming defenses** only viable after Mechanical Pump / Divining Rods built — check water budget before committing.
- **Labyrinth** — CS built day 21 (+150 def). Requires HNB ×2 which are scarce. Good H ratio.
