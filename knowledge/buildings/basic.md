# Buildings — Basic (Day 1–5)

Must-build foundations. Both s19 towns built these in days 1-5.

**Flags:** `T`=temporary · `I`=impervious · `BP-C/U/R/VR`=blueprint · `UP`=upgradable
**Eff:** Def per scarce resource. `A:n`=def÷AP. `W:n`=def÷WR. `H:n`=def÷HNB. Higher=better.
**Resource abbrevs:** `MS`=Metal Support · `PB`=Patchwork Beam · `TP`=Twisted Plank · `WI`=Wrought Iron · `HNB`=Handful of nuts and bolts · `WR`=Water Ration · `SM`=Sheet Metal · `CP`=Copper Pipe · `UCB`=Unshaped Concrete Blocks · `BDG`=Bag of Damp Grass · `WiR`=Wire Reel · `LD`=Laser Diode · `EOC`=Empty Oil Can · `DT`=Duct Tape · `EC`=Electronic component · `PP`=Pharmaceutical Products · `CD`=Compact detonator

## Priority order (both observed towns agree)

Day 1: Workshop, Pump, Watchtower, Soul Purifying Source (free vote → max level by day 3)
Day 2: Defensive Wall, Portal Lock, Butcher (or Factory)
Day 3: Observation Platform (begin free vote chain), Armour Plating, Vegetable Plot, Architect's Study
Day 4: Foundations (unlocks large tree), Eden Project, Plywood, Factory
Day 5: Armour Plating v2, Great Pit (unlock tree), Slip 'n' Slide

## Notes

- Soul Purifying Source free votes: town gets 3 free upgrade votes in days 1-3. Max level ASAP.
- Observation Platform free votes start day 3-4 and continue to Lv.4 (3 AP return discount for returning citizens).
- Great Pit costs 70 AP but unlocks Old-school traps + Spiked pit immediately — worth it day 4-5.
- Slip 'n' Slide: best early def-per-AP with no blueprint. Build day 5 if resources allow.
- Foundations: 30 AP, no def, but gates the entire Foundations tree (Underground city, Labyrinth, Reactor etc). Always day 4-6.
