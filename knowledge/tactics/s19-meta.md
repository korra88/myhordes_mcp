## Season 19 timeline

- Season started approximately 2026-02-21

## Coalition playstyle

A coordinated meta-coalition does **not** necessarily enforce strict Opti rules:
- Professions and skills are advised based on playstyle, not mandated
- Citizens have freedom to choose builds that suit them
- Example: investing in Enduring skills pays off throughout the whole town, rather than holding skill points for Reclusive camping at endgame
- This is distinct from a true Opti town, where directors dictate every choice

Unfilled roster slots are often deliberate — random strangers who aren't on board with the plan can ruin the town. A 33/40 town waiting to fill is gatekeeping, not struggling to recruit.
